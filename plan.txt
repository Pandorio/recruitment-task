Comprehensive renovation of a 15 sqm room, including surface preparation, painting the walls and replacing the floor.

Day 1: Planning and demolition
- Remove old furniture and old flooring
- Check the condition of walls and subfloor
- Calculate budget and plan purchases
- Purchase necessary tools and materials

Day 2: Preparation
- Cover fixtures with plastic sheeting
- Wash walls and ceiling with painter's soap
- Patch wall holes with putty and leave to dry

Day 3: Priming
- Sand patched areas and remove dust
- Apply primer to walls and ceiling

Day 4: Painting
- Apply painter's tape
- Apply two layers of paint, first to the ceiling, then to the walls
- Remove tape and plastic sheeting

Day 5: Flooring
- Level the subfloor with OSB boards
- Lay an acoustic underlay
- Install laminate flooring, keeping expansion gaps
- Install baseboards and a threshold strip

Day 6: Finishing
- Clean the floor
- Perform final inspection
- Move in new furniture
